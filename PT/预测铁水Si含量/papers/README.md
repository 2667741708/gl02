# 论文与研究资料

本目录保存论文书目、阅读笔记和有合法授权的文件。不要把无授权的付费论文全文
复制进仓库；优先保存 DOI、出版页面、摘要笔记、实验设置和对本项目的可验证启发。

## 初始书目

1. Cui Zeqian 等，*Intelligent prediction of silicon content in hot metal of
   blast furnace based on neural network time series model*，2021，
   DOI `10.3969/j.issn.1000-7059.2021.03.007`。
2. Fontes 等，*Blast furnace hot metal temperature and silicon content
   prediction using soft sensor based on fuzzy C-means and exogenous nonlinear
   autoregressive models*，Computers & Chemical Engineering，2020，
   DOI `10.1016/j.compchemeng.2020.107028`。
3. Lughofer 等，*Prediction and Explanation Models for Hot Metal Temperature,
   Silicon Concentration and Cooling Capacity in Ironmaking Blast Furnaces*，
   Steel Research International，2021，DOI `10.1002/srin.202100078`。
4. Liu Xiaojie 等，*Control of silicon content in blast furnace iron based on
   GRA–LSTM–BAS prediction methods*，2024，
   DOI `10.1177/03019233231221676`。
5. Ma Ju'an 等，*Prediction model for silicon content of hot metal in blast
   furnace based on feature selection and SHAP-Transformer*，2025，
   DOI `10.13228/j.boyuan.issn0449-749x.20250088`。
6. Koh 等，*Concept Bottleneck Models*，ICML 2020，
   https://proceedings.mlr.press/v119/koh20a.html 。
7. Agarwal 等，*Neural Additive Models: Interpretable Machine Learning with
   Neural Nets*，2020，https://arxiv.org/abs/2004.13912 。
8. Lim 等，*Temporal Fusion Transformers for Interpretable Multi-horizon
   Time Series Forecasting*，2021，https://arxiv.org/abs/1912.09363 。
9. Nie 等，*A Time Series is Worth 64 Words: Long-term Forecasting with
   Transformers*，2023，https://arxiv.org/abs/2211.14730 。
10. Kim 等，*Higher-order Neural Additive Models*，2022，
    https://arxiv.org/abs/2209.15409 。
